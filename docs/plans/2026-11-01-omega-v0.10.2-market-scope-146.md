## Why

Since 146.0.0 the custom-section arm of `stage_strategy_draft` (and of `preview_strategy_report`'s FIELDS source)
REQUIRES `marketScope`: `anyOf [{enum: all, crypto, l1, memes, defi, tradfi, stocks, indices, commodities}, null]`,
`required: [kind, title, benchmarkTicker, marketScope, notes, columns]`, `additionalProperties: false`. v0.10.1's
`CustomSection.wire()` emitted `benchmarkTicker` and `notes` only, so every plan failed the manager's
`authoring/preflight.py` schema check with `SCHEMA_REQUIRED` at `axes.REPORT.sections[].marketScope` from 06:12Z, and
the manager's hourly panel pull (one custom section to `preview_strategy_report`) was refused live until it named the
key (manager main `466bd0b`, 08:07Z). No new strategy could be planned until the pin moved.

The decisive measurement (the manager's probe, read-only, 2026-11-01T07:58Z, its panel request with `limit: 1`):

- `marketScope: null` -> accepted, one rendered section. **null is a per-coin section**, the shape every section OMEGA
  has ever built.
- `marketScope: "crypto"` -> refused: `[report-column] draft preview section 'custom:...': a market section cannot
  carry this column — metric 'PRIOR_TPO_POC' is not read on a market section's bars`. **A named scope is a different
  kind of section** (a MARKET section), for the market metrics 146 added.

So the default is `None`, always emitted, and a named scope is an authoring choice OMEGA exposes but never selects.

## The extraction script was NOT run, by decision

`scripts/refresh_metric_contracts.py` reads `get_metric_construction_hints` per metric over the mcporter CLI, then
`scripts/build_corpus.py` and the render-coverage sweep measure every metric x transform mechanism live, and a
re-extraction record pins what rendered. That is the v0.11 re-extraction at 146: 24 hint reads under the 3 req/s
rate limit, a corpus rebuild, the `MKT_*` family's first renders on a MARKET section (a shape no sweep has sent), and
`CROWD_*_Z` beside the existing `CROWD_*` reads - several hours, with the platform one hour out of a 58-minute outage
and the manager's writes just unblocked. This patch is the smallest change that makes a plan compile at 146.0.0;
the manager was blocked on it. The 24 stay refused offline until that extraction, which is a separate change.
