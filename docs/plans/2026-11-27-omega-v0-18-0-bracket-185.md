# OMEGA v0.18.0 at contract 185.2.0: the trade bracket replaces the trade level menu

**Record, 2026-11-27.** Contract 185.2.0, build `ede1fce5` (`GET /mcp/version` read 02:29:41Z and 02:33:11Z; it did not
move). Previous pin: v0.17.0 at 184.3.2. Contract 185.0.0 arrived 2026-11-26 09:49:54Z; battlegrid-manager blocked
strategy writes on it (a scoped block) because `stage_strategy_draft` no longer takes `tradeLevelMenu`.

**The result in one line:** TRADE_LEVEL_POLICY requires `tradeBracket` - per direction ONE stop and ONE exit plan - in
place of `tradeLevelMenu` (1 - 8 stops, 1 - 8 exit plans). Every OMEGA plan now stages a bracket, every rule of a menu
that still applies to one stop and one plan is kept, and one rule is new: an ATR stop outside the strategy's own band is
refused at commit.

## What the platform moved (184.12.0 -> 185.2.0, the 64 tools the manager calls)

- `stage_strategy_draft` input: `/axes/TRADE_LEVEL_POLICY/tradeLevelMenu` removed, `/axes/TRADE_LEVEL_POLICY/tradeBracket`
  added and required (`$defs/directionTradeBracket` = {stop, exitPlan}, additionalProperties false at every level). The
  stop arms (ATR `atrMultiple` (0, 5] | LEVEL {level, bufferAtrMultiple [0, 5]} | PERCENT (0, 100)) and `$defs/exitPlan`
  are the menu's, unchanged.
- `get_strategy` and `commit_strategy_draft` output: `strategy.tradeLevelMenu` removed, `strategy.tradeBracket` added and
  required.
- `preview_strategy_trade_levels` output: per direction one `stop`, one `setup` (or null) and a `refusal` (or null), in
  place of `stopCandidates`, `setups`, `rejectedPairs`, `counters`. OMEGA parses no preview.
- `$defs/metricKey`: LIQUIDITY_POOL_ABOVE_AGE and LIQUIDITY_POOL_BELOW_AGE added (588 keys).
- Vendor pack 32.0.118 (text, not measured here): a stage still sending `tradeLevelMenu` is refused as an unrecognised
  key; `commit_strategy_draft` refuses an ATR stop outside minStopLossAtrMultiple - maxStopLossAtrMultiple (both
  inclusive), naming `tradeBracket.<DIRECTION>.stop.atrMultiple`; every earlier stop and exit-plan refusal stands.

## What changed in OMEGA

- `omega/trade_levels.py`
  - `validate_bracket(bracket, *, dials, sections, anchor, schema=None, trailing_enabled=None)`: the bracket judged
    whole - the write schema's walk first (TRADE_MENU_SHAPE; a menu-shaped value fails it), then per direction the band
    rule (new), a LEVEL stop by the level rule, the one exit plan by every exit-plan rule, a level both stop and leg.
  - `default_bracket(min_rr, schema=None, *, stop_range=None)` (with `default_bracket_stop`, `default_bracket_target`,
    `default_bracket_basis`): the platform's ATR 1.5 / 3R when it fits; else the stop is OMEGA's fallback (the 0.1 ATR
    step nearest the band's midpoint strictly inside it, else the lower bound, else the upper) and the plan one leg at the
    minimum when the minimum is above 3R (the v0.14.3 R-target rule reduced to one plan: its farthest target).
  - `bracket_required`, `menu_schema` / `MENU_SCHEMA` (the 184.3.2 capture), `DEFAULT_PROBES`, `PLATFORM_BRACKET_*`;
    `atr_stop_bounds` reads the bracket's stop arm; `_plan_findings` is the exit-plan rules on one plan (the menu's loop
    calls it).
  - History kept: `validate_menu`, `default_menu`, `default_stops`, `menu_form`, `list_bounds`, `migrate_menu` judge a
    menu against MENU_SCHEMA when no schema is named; nothing on the generation path calls them. `migrate_menu` is no
    path to a bracket - OMEGA never converts a menu.
- `omega/generate.py`
  - `Thesis.trade_bracket` at the position of `trade_level_menu` (removed, not aliased: passing it is a TypeError).
  - `StrategyPlan.trade_bracket()`, `bracket_findings()`, `update_bracket(current)`, `update_bracket_findings(current)`;
    `menu_findings()` / `update_menu_findings(current)` remain as v0.18.0 aliases of the new two.
  - `axes()` stages `{minAtrPct, minStopLossAtrMultiple, maxStopLossAtrMultiple, minRiskRewardRatio, tradeBracket}`.
  - `axes_update(current)` carries the live `tradeBracket` verbatim (a copy) unless the thesis names one; a `current`
    without one - or with only a 184 `tradeLevelMenu` - raises `BracketUnknown` (a ValueError). The findings never raise:
    `update_bracket_findings` answers TRADE_BRACKET_LIVE_UNKNOWN, staged or not.
  - The critique line is `trade bracket: ...` and names where the bracket came from.
- Corpus: the 185.2.0 capture and sidecar, the manifest's `writeSurface` (184.3.2 under `supersedes`), the two keys in
  `_unmeasured_108.json`, `data/derived/condition_vocabulary_108.json` re-derived (source, 588 keys), the audit record.
- `docs/02-transform-layer.md` regenerated (it names the capture); `docs/01` regenerated unchanged.
- `pyproject.toml`: 0.18.0.
