# OMEGA v0.14.0 at contract 171.0.0: the trade level menu's exit plans

**Record, 2026-11-12.** Contract 171.0.0, build `588e449a` (`GET /mcp/version` before and after every live step).
Previous pin: v0.13.1 at 169.4.0 (released 08:18Z the same morning). Contracts 170 and 171 are undocumented: the vendor
README's newest entry is v169.4.

## What moved

- **170.0.0** (first served 08:25Z) replaced the trade level menu's profit side. Per direction, `targets` (1 - 8 of
  R_MULTIPLE / LEVEL) is REMOVED and `exitPlans` is REQUIRED: 1 - 8 plans, each `{targets: [1 - 4 legs {method:
  R_MULTIPLE {multiple} | LEVEL {level}, closePct: an integer 0 - 100}]}`. A position can scale out. It applies to
  `stage_strategy_draft` input, and to `get_strategy` and `commit_strategy_draft` output. The platform migrated every
  stored strategy one to one: each old target became a plan of one leg closing 100% (read on all 16 account
  strategies at 08:55Z). Gate blocks and signal logs now name an `exitPlan` and a `reason` (`TARGETS_OUT_OF_ORDER`,
  `RISK_REWARD_TOO_LOW`, `TARGET_LEG_BELOW_ORDER_FLOOR`).
- **171.0.0** (about 09:13Z) left the six draft tools byte-identical to 170's (the 09:16Z listing). Four exit-plan
  probes staged again at 171 answered as at 170.

Every plan OMEGA v0.13.x emits fails the 170 stage schema. The manager's plan #74 failed offline on exactly the menu,
so authoring failed closed until this release.

## What was measured

32 create stages at 170.0.0, between 09:02 and 09:11Z. 26 drafts were staged, read back (each kept as sent) and
discarded; none was committed. 6 were refused at input. Record: `data/audit/exit_plans_170_2026-11-12.json`.

| Rule | Probes |
| --- | --- |
| Refused at input: legs closing more than 100% together ("The close percentages of an exit plan sum to at most 100") | X4 60 + 60, X5 50 + 100 |
| Refused at input: a first leg closing 0% ("An exit plan's first target closes a share") | X7 |
| Refused at input by the schema: 5 legs, 9 plans, closePct 101 | X13, X14, X16 |
| Accepted: legs closing under 100% (the rest runs), a later 0% leg, a level leg, repeated plans, mixed shapes | X3, X6, Y8, X15, Y10, X17, X18 |
| A draft error: an R leg not farther than the R leg immediately before it ("targets run away from entry") | X8, X9, X11, Z4; a level leg between them breaks the comparison (Z3) |
| A draft error: an all-R plan paying less than `minRiskRewardRatio`, where it pays the closePct-weighted mean of its legs' R over the share it closes ("The LONG exit plan pays 1.3R") | Y1 - Y4, Y6, Y7 refused; X10 (2R), Y5 (1.8R), Y8 (1.5R exactly) accepted |
| Not judged at stage: a plan with a level leg (its R is known only at a decision) | Z1, Z2, Z3 |
| A draft error: one level as a stop and a leg of the same direction | Y9 |

## Where OMEGA holds it

- **`omega/trade_levels.py` follows the write schema it is given (the pinned 171 capture by default).**
  - `menu_form` returns `exitPlans` at 170+ and `targets` at 168; `list_bounds("exitPlans" | "legs")`.
  - `validate_menu` judges exit plans (`_exit_plan_findings`) with these codes:
    - `TRADE_MENU_EXIT_PLAN_OVER_100`
    - `TRADE_MENU_EXIT_PLAN_FIRST_LEG_EMPTY`
    - `TRADE_MENU_EXIT_PLAN_OUT_OF_ORDER`
    - `TRADE_MENU_EXIT_PLAN_BELOW_MIN_RR`
    - `TRADE_MENU_EXIT_PLAN_JUDGED_PER_DECISION` (a warning)
  - `plan_pays` is the stage's arithmetic.
  - The 168 probes replay against the 168.3.0 capture (`schema=`).
- **`default_menu`** at 170 is the platform's migration shape: one one-leg plan per R target, closing 100%. It is
  exactly the menu probe X1 staged clean.
- **`migrate_menu`**: a thesis menu still in 168's `targets` form stages as the platform migrated stored menus, on a
  CREATE and on an UPDATE. A live strategy's menu is carried as the platform holds it.
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_exit_plans_170.py` replays every probe on the field the platform named.
  - `tests/test_trade_level_menu_168.py` keeps the 168 replays.

## Reviewed

An independent review (2026-11-12) found no fail-open hole in the exit-plan rules and no regression on the 168 path.
It raised two points:

- **A menu side carrying both `targets` and `exitPlans` was migrated, silently replacing its exit plans.** Fixed:
  only a side with `targets` and no `exitPlans` is migrated, and the schema walk refuses a side in both forms.
- **A one-leg level plan carries no "judged per decision" warning.** Kept as is: it is the platform's own migration
  shape for every live strategy's band targets, and a 168 level target carried none.

Both are pinned in `tests/test_exit_plans_170.py`. One more rule came from replaying the record: a plan out of order is
judged on its order alone. X11 pays 1.35R, below the minimum, and the stage named only the order.

## Not done here

- No preset scales out. The default stays the migration shape, and a scale-out plan is the author's to write.
- What a decision does with an exit plan (`TARGET_LEG_BELOW_ORDER_FLOOR`, the per-pair order and R:R of a plan with a
  level leg) is runtime, judged per coin by the platform. OMEGA warns and does not model it.
